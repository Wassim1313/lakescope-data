LakeScope privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026 (app 0.4.0). Publisher: Atlas Labs. Contact: atlaslabsdaba@gmail.com.

Short version

You can use LakeScope without an account. Lake conditions, the map and the 3-D view need nothing from you. If you share a catch, like one or buy PRO, the app creates an anonymous account (no email) so those work; signing in with Google is optional. We never sell personal data. The app shows rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one.

Location

With your permission the app uses your approximate location on your phone to centre the map and show the best lakes near you. To show catches near you, your position is sent to our server for that one request and is not stored. You can refuse or withdraw the permission at any time; the rest of the app works without it.

Sharing catches (optional)

If you share a catch, we store the photo, species, length and weight you enter, the lake, the time (rounded to the hour) and the water and weather conditions of that moment. Before storing the photo our server re-encodes it to a small image and removes all camera data, including the GPS position; the original file is discarded. Your exact position is never stored: we keep only the centre of a grid square of about 1 km, or only the lake if you choose "Hide where on the lake" (the default on small lakes). Shared catches, your display name and like counts are public in LakeScope, on LakeScope web pages and in share links.

Accounts

To post, like or report, the app creates an anonymous account (a random id and a key kept on your phone). You can choose to sign in with Google (for example on the LakeScope website) to use the same account and PRO on your phone and the web. If you do, we receive your Google account id and email address. We use them only to link your devices, restore purchases and answer you if you contact us; your email is never shown to others and never used for marketing.

Purchases

PRO (Lake Pass and subscriptions) is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own terms; we never see card details. We store which product you bought, its expiry date and the Google Play purchase token, and check it with Google to unlock PRO on every device linked to your account.

Ads

LakeScope shows a rewarded video ad only when you tap "Watch a short video" to unlock one lake for 24 hours. The ad is served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID and IP address to serve and measure the ad (non-personalised ads are requested). See Google's privacy policy. We store only that the unlock happened, for 24 hours.

Moderation

If you report a post we store the reason and your account id. Posts that break the community rules are hidden or removed; accounts that repeatedly break them are suspended. You can block any user.

Favourites, catch log and alerts

Your favourite lakes, your private catch log and the last downloaded lake data stay on your phone. Lake alerts are made on your phone from the public daily lake update; there is no push service.

Retention and deletion

Posts stay until you delete them or your account. You can delete your account and everything linked to it in the app (About > Account and privacy > Delete my account) or at https://lakescope-api-production.up.railway.app/delete-account. Server request logs (IP address, time) are kept for up to 30 days to prevent abuse. Our servers run on Railway (United States); lake data files are served by GitHub Pages and map tiles by OpenFreeMap.

Children

LakeScope is not directed at children under 13. Catch sharing is for users 13 and older.

Safety notice

LakeScope is not an official safety service. Lake levels, ramps and ice can change quickly: always check with the lake office and follow posted signs and local officials. Fishing times and spots are suggestions, not promises. Depth lines are approximate and not for navigation.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
